AIagentAbuse is an independent, open record of real-world incidents involving AI agents, paired with practical defenses.

## Why

AI agents are being given real permissions: inboxes, code repositories, cloud accounts, and payment methods. When they fail or are manipulated, the lessons are scattered across blog posts, advisories, and social media threads. This site collects those lessons in one consistent, sourced format.

## Who it is for

Engineers building agents, security teams assessing them, and anyone deciding how much to trust one.

## Principles

- **Sourced, not speculative.** Every entry cites public sources.
- **Human-reviewed.** Automated tools may help find and draft entries. A human checks everything before it is marked verified.
- **Defensive only.** We explain what went wrong and how to prevent it. We do not publish attack code.
- **Private by default.** No cookies, no trackers, no third-party scripts. See [privacy](/privacy/).
- **Open.** Content is CC BY 4.0, code is MIT, and the full history is public.
