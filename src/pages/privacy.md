**Short version: this site sets no cookies, runs no trackers, and loads nothing from third parties.**

- **Cookies:** none.
- **Analytics:** none at present. If we enable Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookie-free and does not track individuals, we will update this page first.
- **Search:** runs entirely in your browser against a static index. Your queries are not sent anywhere.
- **Fonts and scripts:** all served from this domain.
- **Hosting:** the site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare processes standard request data (such as IP address) to deliver and protect the site, under its own privacy policy.
- **Submissions:** made on GitHub, under GitHub's privacy policy. Anything you post in an issue is public.
- **Email digest:** not live. When it launches, this page will say which provider stores your address and how to unsubscribe.
